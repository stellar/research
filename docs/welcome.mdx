# Research at the Stellar Development Foundation

The [Stellar Development Foundation](https://stellar.org) supports technological and scientific research through its in-house research team and its academic grants program.

:::tip We're hiring a research intern — starting ASAP
We are looking for a [research intern in formal methods](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/stellar/32e1d08d-76a9-4561-b6b7-c149799da1a8) to combine LLMs and formal methods to model a subsystem of stellar-core and prove (or disprove) the absence of denial-of-service attacks, in the vein of our [CAV 2026](https://conferences.i-cav.org/2026/) paper ["Show Me The Money: An Exercise in Proof-Driven Software Understanding"](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-032-32537-2_11).
The project lasts 3 to 4 months and we'd like to start as soon as possible. **[Apply here](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/stellar/32e1d08d-76a9-4561-b6b7-c149799da1a8)**, and see the [internships page](/internships) for more about our internship program.
:::

:::tip We're hiring a Director of Research
We are also looking for a [Director of Research](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/stellar/4cada7d9-0387-45d4-8965-6783690cf720) to lead SDF's research team and academic grants program. **[Apply here](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/stellar/4cada7d9-0387-45d4-8965-6783690cf720)**.
:::
